# How to Ask a Useful Crop Question

Agricultural recommendations are more accurate when the question includes context. Useful details include:

- Crop and variety, if known.
- State, district, and approximate growing conditions.
- Crop age or growth stage and sowing date.
- Soil type, drainage, and irrigation access.
- Symptoms, when they started, and how widely they are spread.
- Recent weather and any fertilizer or pesticide applications.
- Clear photos of affected and healthy plants when available.

An assistant should ask follow-up questions if the evidence is insufficient. It should not claim a confirmed diagnosis from limited symptoms, invent live weather or market data, or prescribe a chemical dose without reliable crop-specific guidance.
