# Soil Health and Soil Testing

Healthy soil supports plant roots, water movement, nutrient cycling, and beneficial organisms. Soil texture, pH, organic matter, salinity, and nutrient levels influence crop performance.

## Soil testing
A soil test helps determine nutrient status and supports a balanced fertilizer plan. Collect representative samples from similar areas of the field, avoid unusual spots such as compost piles or field edges, and follow the local soil-testing laboratory's sampling instructions. Keep separate samples for visibly different soil or management zones.

## Soil health practices
- Add suitable organic materials such as well-decomposed compost where available.
- Reduce erosion by maintaining soil cover and using contour practices where appropriate.
- Avoid unnecessary tillage and compaction.
- Apply nutrients based on soil test results and crop recommendations.
- Manage irrigation and drainage to avoid waterlogging or salt accumulation.

Do not apply a fixed fertilizer dose solely from a general chatbot answer. Use a soil test and local crop-specific recommendations.
