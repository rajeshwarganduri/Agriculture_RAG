# Balanced Nutrient Management

Plants need nutrients in appropriate amounts and proportions. Nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium are major nutrients; secondary and micronutrients may also be important depending on soil and crop conditions. Deficiency symptoms can overlap with disease, salinity, or water stress.

## Better fertilizer decisions
- Use a recent soil test where possible.
- Follow crop-, region-, and season-specific fertilizer recommendations.
- Consider nutrient sources, timing, placement, irrigation, and crop growth stage.
- Avoid excessive application, which can waste money and contribute to environmental losses.
- Store fertilizers safely and keep them away from children, animals, and water sources.

A generic nutrient dose should not replace a local soil-test-based recommendation. If symptoms appear, verify the cause before adding nutrients.
