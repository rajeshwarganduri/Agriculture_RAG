# Agricultural Advisories and Farmer Support

Agricultural advice, subsidy eligibility, insurance terms, and support schemes can change over time and differ by state, district, crop, and applicant category.

## Reliable sources
- State agriculture department and district agriculture office.
- Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) and agricultural university extension services.
- Official government scheme portals and published notifications.
- Soil testing laboratories and recognized plant clinics.
- Official meteorological services for weather warnings and forecasts.

Before applying for a scheme, verify current eligibility, required documents, deadlines, and application process on the official source. This sample knowledge base is educational and does not contain live scheme status or guarantee eligibility.
