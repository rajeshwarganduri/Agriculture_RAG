# Weather-Aware Farm Planning

Weather affects sowing, irrigation, fertilizer application, pest and disease pressure, harvesting, drying, and storage. A forecast is uncertain and should be checked again as conditions change.

## Using weather information
- Check official local forecasts and agricultural advisories.
- Consider rainfall probability, temperature, wind, and warnings, not just a single daily temperature.
- Avoid spraying when wind or rainfall conditions conflict with the approved product label or local advice.
- Plan irrigation around expected rainfall but verify soil moisture.
- Protect harvested produce from rain and moisture where possible.
- Follow official warnings for storms, heat, floods, or other severe conditions.

This knowledge base does not provide live weather. For current decisions, check an official meteorological service and local agricultural advisory.
