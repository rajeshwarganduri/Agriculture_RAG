# Seed Quality and Sowing

Good establishment begins with suitable seed, correct sowing time, proper seed rate, and appropriate sowing depth and spacing. These values differ by crop, variety, region, and production system.

## Seed and sowing checklist
- Select a variety recommended for the local agro-climatic zone and season.
- Prefer certified or otherwise reliable seed with clear labelling.
- Check seed lot, germination information, and expiry or validity details where applicable.
- Prepare a suitable seedbed with adequate moisture and drainage.
- Follow the official crop package of practices for seed rate, spacing, and depth.
- Use seed treatment only when recommended for the crop and purpose, following the product label and safety precautions.

Do not use a universal seed rate or treatment recipe for all crops.
