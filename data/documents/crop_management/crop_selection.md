# Crop Selection and Crop Rotation

## Choosing a crop
Crop selection depends on local climate, season, soil type, water availability, expected market demand, input costs, and the farmer’s available equipment. Before sowing, check local agricultural university or extension guidance and the current seasonal advisory.

## Crop rotation
Crop rotation means growing different crops in sequence on the same field across seasons. Rotating crops with different nutrient needs and root systems can support soil structure, help manage some crop-specific pests and diseases, and diversify farm income. Including legumes may contribute nitrogen to the cropping system, but the benefit depends on species, biomass, and management.

## Practical checklist
1. Identify soil type and drainage.
2. Check rainfall outlook and irrigation access.
3. Choose a locally recommended variety and sowing window.
4. Use certified seed from a reliable source.
5. Plan rotation based on local pest, disease, and nutrient issues.

Recommendations are region- and season-specific; confirm choices with a local agriculture extension officer.
