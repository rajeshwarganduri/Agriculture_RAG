# Irrigation and Water Management

Irrigation scheduling depends on crop stage, soil type, weather, rainfall, and the irrigation method. Both water stress and excess water can reduce yield.

## Efficient practices
- Check soil moisture and crop condition before irrigating.
- Irrigate at locally recommended critical growth stages.
- Repair leaks and maintain channels or drip lines.
- Use mulch where suitable to reduce evaporation and weeds.
- Ensure drainage for crops sensitive to waterlogging.
- Consider drip or sprinkler systems when suitable for the crop, water quality, and farm economics.

## Important caution
There is no single irrigation interval that fits every crop and region. Use local weather forecasts, soil moisture, crop-stage guidance, and extension recommendations to determine timing and quantity.
