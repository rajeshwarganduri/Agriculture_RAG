# Recognizing Plant Disease Symptoms

Plant symptoms such as leaf spots, wilting, yellowing, powdery growth, rotting, or stunted growth can have multiple causes, including fungi, bacteria, viruses, insects, nutrient deficiencies, water stress, and chemical injury. A symptom alone is not a definitive diagnosis.

## First checks
- Note crop and variety, crop age, and when symptoms began.
- Check whether symptoms affect one plant, patches, field edges, or the whole field.
- Inspect both sides of leaves, stems, roots where practical, and nearby plants.
- Review recent rainfall, irrigation, fertilizer, and pesticide use.
- Photograph the affected plant and healthy comparison plants.

Avoid applying pesticides until the cause is reasonably identified. Seek local plant clinic or extension support for fast-spreading, severe, or unfamiliar symptoms.
