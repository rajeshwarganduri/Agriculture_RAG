# Harvest and Post-Harvest Storage

Harvest timing and storage conditions affect quality, shelf life, and losses. Correct maturity indicators vary by crop and intended use.

## General good practices
- Harvest at the recommended maturity stage and handle produce gently.
- Separate damaged, diseased, or contaminated produce where appropriate.
- Keep storage areas clean, dry, ventilated, and suitable for the commodity.
- Prevent contact with standing water and protect produce from pests.
- Dry grains to the crop- and storage-appropriate moisture level using locally recommended guidance before long-term storage.
- Monitor stored products for moisture, insects, mould, and unusual odours.

Storage temperature, humidity, and moisture targets are commodity-specific. Use official crop-specific recommendations rather than a single value for all produce.
